# 29 — Lead the report with the total, the questions, and the next action

**What to build:** The report answers the three questions a Buyer has while standing at a desk — what this actually costs, what to ask, and what to do next — before any supporting detail.

**Blocked by:** 26 — Carry the Buyer from a verified offer to the report and back.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The out-the-door total, the three most material findings, and one next action appear first, without scrolling, at 360 px width.
- [ ] Each leading amount links to its source and calculation rather than restating it.
- [ ] The question list is source-grounded, Buyer-editable, and copyable as text.
- [ ] Amounts carry their precision, and unknown values are labeled as unknown rather than rendered as zero or omitted.
- [ ] The PDF export presents the same ordering and the same values as the HTML report, from the same immutable payload.
- [ ] Time from report open to a correctly identified out-the-door total is instrumented as a pilot measure.
- [ ] Automated accessibility checks cover the leading section, including heading order, contrast, and keyboard reachability.
