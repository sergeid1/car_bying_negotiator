# 32 — Publish an honest sample report and a defined one-time offer

**What to build:** A prospective Buyer can see exactly what the product returns before paying, and can buy it at a stated one-time price with stated terms.

**Blocked by:** 29 — Lead the report with the total, the questions, and the next action.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The sample is a redacted real report produced from a permissioned case, labeled as an example, with the redaction method recorded.
- [ ] The offer is a one-time per-purchase price presented at a defined point in the flow; subscriptions and success fees remain excluded per specification §2.2.
- [ ] The price is recorded as an experiment value with its approval, not copied from a competitor's advertised price.
- [ ] Copy avoids "AI negotiator" and "safe to sign", and does not attribute savings to the product; a verified quote change is described as a quote change.
- [ ] Refund terms, scope limits, the pilot jurisdiction, and the excluded purchase types are stated before payment.
- [ ] What the Buyer receives free and what requires payment are unambiguous before any document is uploaded.
