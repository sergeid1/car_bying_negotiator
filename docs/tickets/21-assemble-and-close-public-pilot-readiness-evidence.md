# 21 — Assemble and close public-pilot readiness evidence

**What to build:** A reviewable readiness record shows that the product's jurisdiction, provider, corpus, policy, privacy, legal-copy, and measurement prerequisites are either approved for the bounded pilot or explicitly block release.

**Blocked by:** 02 — Establish golden fixtures and contract evaluation; 06 — Extract Deal Terms with field-level evidence; 09 — Produce a transparent Market Assessment; 19 — Capture privacy-minimized feedback and pilot metrics.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] One pilot jurisdiction is selected and represented by an effective-dated, sourced, versioned pack reviewed by qualified counsel.
- [ ] Automotive and processing provider terms document display, caching, attribution, derived-value, retention, deletion, training, regional-processing, coverage, and cost rights.
- [ ] At least 100 permissioned representative Dealer documents are acquired, de-identified as appropriate, and split before evaluation into development and frozen held-out subsets.
- [ ] A consumer-side automotive expert validates opening, target, and walk-away policy behavior against representative retrospective cases.
- [ ] Upload limits, materiality tolerances, confidence thresholds, market freshness/sample rules, and report-schema fixtures are frozen and versioned.
- [ ] Recovery mechanism, infrastructure/provider choices, provider inventory, purposes, subprocessors, regions, retention, and deletion behavior are documented.
- [ ] Legal and product copy avoids unsupported law, advice, safety, fraud, guarantee, and savings claims and discloses relevant commercial relationships.
- [ ] Pilot success and safety thresholds are preregistered before pilot outcomes are reviewed; any unmet prerequisite explicitly blocks release.
- [ ] The readiness record includes reviewed Flyway migrations, Hibernate schema-validation results, entity ownership and fetch rules, database constraints, and evidence that no production schema depends on Hibernate auto-generation.
