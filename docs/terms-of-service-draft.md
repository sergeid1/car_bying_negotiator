# Terms of Service — draft pending counsel review

This is a product-policy draft for ticket 123, pending counsel review. It is not final Terms of Service.

otdog serves the Buyer and is free for Buyers. Companies shown in insurance quotes or loan
pre-approvals may pay otdog for quotes or pre-approvals obtained through the platform. Payment never
changes provider inclusion, quote order, wording, findings, or the Desk Check. Quotes from providers
that pay nothing, including quotes entered by the Buyer, are compared on the same terms.
Insurance quotes are ordered by premium at the same coverage; pre-approvals are ordered by total
cost. Different insurance coverage is labelled rather than ranked together.

No dealer or dealer group pays otdog for anything. otdog accepts no compensation from
dealer-arranged financing, dealer reserve, or products sold at the desk. If the dealer's financing
costs less than the outside pre-approval on comparable terms, the comparison says so.

With the Buyer's consent, an insurance application goes only to the licensed comparison partner
and insurers quoting through it. A loan request goes to the licensed partner and lenders quoting
through it under the Buyer's written authorization for a soft inquiry. The Buyer continues on
the provider's site where applicable. Provider conditions and expiry remain part of the offer.
otdog does not send application information or Buyer contact details to dealers. The Buyer chooses
what to send to a dealer from their own email and makes every purchase decision.

Counsel review remains needed for final service terms, consent wording, partner roles, and the
provider continuation experience. This draft makes no legal conclusion or promise of savings.
