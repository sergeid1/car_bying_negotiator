# Permissioned case studies

No real case studies have been published. Synthetic fixtures and development checkout
records are not real purchase evidence. Published studies belong only in
`docs/case-studies/published/*.md`; the template is outside that directory.

Before publication, obtain explicit Buyer permission for the exact proposed text,
audience, channels, and duration. Service consent, feedback, research consent, or a
purchase does not grant publication permission. Keep the signed permission and source
evidence in the controlled release process, never this repository. Use an opaque
publication permission reference, with no Buyer or case identity. Permission must be
current at publication; withdrawal requires removing the study from every controlled
publication channel and recording the removal in the controlled process.

Every published study must use the template's machine-checked fields: an affirmative
permission flag and reference, an ISO permission date, a review window, numeric
numerator and positive denominator, and substantive evidence/limitations sections.
Include separate denominators for each additional rate; identify selection criteria,
refunds, missing responses, attrition, and the limits of any single-case example.
The publication check is a structural gate; a human must verify permission, source
evidence, redaction, and every claim before publishing.

Keep Buyer-reported outcomes explicitly unverified and separate from verified
document evidence. Survey willingness to pay is separate from actual paid purchases;
development no-charge checkout does not establish revenue. State what was not measured,
including causation, when applicable. Do not infer verified savings from self-report or
claim the service caused an observed difference. Include no Buyer, Dealer, VIN, case,
document, contact, or purchase identifiers. A published aggregate does not authorize
retaining a Buyer case graph or overriding account deletion/export rules.
