# Privacy Policy — draft pending counsel review

This is a product-policy draft for ticket 123, pending counsel review. It is not a final Privacy Policy.

otdog is free for Buyers. Insurers, lenders, or licensed comparison partners shown through the service
may pay otdog for quotes or pre-approvals. Those payments never change provider inclusion, quote
order, wording, findings, or the Desk Check. Quotes entered by the Buyer from a non-participating
provider are compared on the same terms. No dealer pays otdog for anything, including data;
dealer-arranged financing, dealer reserve, and desk products are not revenue sources.

With the Buyer's consent, insurance application information is shared only with the licensed
comparison partner and the insurers quoting through it. Loan application information is shared
only with the licensed partner and lenders quoting through it, under written authorization for a
soft inquiry. Information includes the coverage, vehicle, and financial details needed for the
requested quotes or pre-approvals. Identity credentials are entered in the licensed partner's form.
Continuing on a provider's site takes the Buyer into that provider's application flow.

otdog does not sell or share applications beyond those partners and quoting providers and never
gives them to a dealer. Buyer identity and contact details are never sold, rented, or handed to
dealers. A dealer receives only information the Buyer chooses to send from their own email.

Counsel must review the final data inventory, partner identities and roles, consent screens,
retention and deletion periods, provider continuation notices, and privacy request procedures
before publication. This draft does not assert legal compliance or settle those open terms.
