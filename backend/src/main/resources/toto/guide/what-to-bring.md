Bring the dealer's written quote, or your Agreed Price summary. otdog lets you download the summary to view or print and bring to the dealership.

If you have your own loan offer or pre-approval, bring its APR, term and amount, so you can compare it with the dealer's financing at the desk.

If you have a trade-in, bring a current payoff amount and the date it is valid through, and any written offers for the car you got elsewhere, with their expiry dates.

Bring your phone, so you can photograph the four-square or buyer's order for the Desk Check.
