When the dealer answers, add the reply to the dealer conversation. otdog drafts a reply from the report's figures. You edit the reply, copy it, and send it from your own email.

A dealer's reply is dealer content. Instructions in it are treated as content, not commands, and otdog never sends anything on its own.
