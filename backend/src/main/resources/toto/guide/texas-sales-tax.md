For a Texas resident buying from a Texas licensed dealer, otdog applies the Texas motor vehicle sales tax of 6.25% to the taxable price after any trade-in allowance. There is no local motor vehicle tax and no cap.

On an example $30,000 price with no trade-in, the tax is $1,875.00. With a $12,000 trade-in, $18,000 is taxable and the tax is $1,125.00.

Texas does not tax a separately stated documentary fee or vehicle inventory tax reimbursement. Nor does it tax a separately stated service contract, warranty, maintenance agreement, roadside assistance, GAP or other debt cancellation agreement, insurance, or auto club membership.

Dealer preparation and other dealer charges are taxed, and so are accessories attached at the sale. A lump total of dealer charges or add-ons has no labels, so otdog taxes all of it; itemized paperwork is needed for a charge to be left out.
