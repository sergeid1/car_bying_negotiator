Sometimes the dealer's APR is below your own loan offer. When it is, otdog says so plainly and shows how much less the dealer's financing costs in scheduled interest at the worksheet's amount financed and term.

This holds even for a pre-approval you got through otdog. What any lender pays otdog never changes this comparison.
