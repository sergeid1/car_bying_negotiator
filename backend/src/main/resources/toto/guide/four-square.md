A four-square is a worksheet a dealer may use at the desk. It shows four figures in boxes: the price, the trade-in, the down payment, and the monthly payment. It often also shows a loan term and an APR.

A four-square can lead with the monthly payment while the out-the-door total and the loan term are missing. Payment-led figures hide the total, so ask for an itemized out-the-door total and the term in months.

At the dealership, you can photograph the four-square. otdog checks it against the price you agreed, including your trade-in, and names common worksheet tactics it finds, each with a question you can ask at the desk.
