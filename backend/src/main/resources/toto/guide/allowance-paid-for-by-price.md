A dealer can offer a high trade-in allowance while raising the price of the car you are buying. The larger allowance can look generous while the price increase takes it back.

When the trade allowance is above its reference and the price is above the price you agreed, otdog shows the net effect of the two together: whether you come out ahead or behind.
