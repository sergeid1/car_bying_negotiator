For a Florida resident buying from a Florida dealer, otdog applies the 6% state sales tax to the taxable price after any trade-in allowance, plus the discretionary sales surtax of the county where the car will be registered.

The county surtax applies only to the first $5,000 of the taxable price after the trade-in. On an example $35,000 car in a county with a 1% surtax and no trade-in, the state tax is $2,100 and the county surtax is $50, for $2,150 in all.

County surtax rates differ. Citrus and Collier counties have none, Hamilton County's is 2%, and Martin and Palm Beach counties' is 0.5% in 2026. otdog finds the county from your registration ZIP code, and asks you to choose when a ZIP code spans more than one county.

otdog's Florida rules tax the selling price, dealer fees and add-ons. Government charges such as title and registration are not taxed. otdog does not yet check title, registration, plate, tire or battery fees.
