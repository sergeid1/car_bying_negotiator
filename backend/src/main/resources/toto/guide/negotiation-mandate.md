A Negotiation Mandate sets your limits for any later contact with a dealer about a car: your target price, your walk-away ceiling, and what may be shared. Approving it sends nothing to any dealer.

Under a mandate, otdog drafts messages asking a dealer for a written, itemized out-the-door quote, and proposes prices at or below your target and never above your walk-away ceiling.

The mandate limits what you can approve. otdog never sends anything: you approve each message and send it from your own email. You can revoke the mandate at any time.
