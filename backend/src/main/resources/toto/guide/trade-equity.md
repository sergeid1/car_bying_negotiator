Trade equity is the trade-in allowance minus what you still owe on the car. If the allowance is more than the payoff, you have positive equity, which can go toward the car you are buying.

If you owe more than the allowance, you have negative equity. A dealer can add it to the new loan, which raises the payment and the interest you pay. otdog shows trade equity once the payoff is known.

A trade-in shortfall is a trade figure, never a discount on the car. otdog does not buy cars or send your trade anywhere.
