Every email to a dealer goes from your own email address, after you approve that exact message. otdog adds nothing to it.

You can send a message from your own email app: copy the approved draft, or open it pre-filled in your mail app, then send it there.

Or you can connect Gmail or Outlook with a send-only permission, and otdog sends the approved draft through your account. otdog does not read your mailbox. Dealer replies come in only when you paste, forward or upload them.
