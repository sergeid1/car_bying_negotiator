The Desk Check compares the paperwork at the dealer's desk with the price you agreed. You photograph or upload the four-square or buyer's order, and otdog shows any difference from your Agreed Price, including your trade-in.

otdog reads the paperwork and shows you what to ask. It never contacts the dealer; you decide what to say.

Paperwork terms that otdog has read but you have not yet confirmed are shown as read, not verified. Check each one against the page before relying on it.
