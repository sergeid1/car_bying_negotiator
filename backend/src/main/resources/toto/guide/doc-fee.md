A doc fee, also called a documentation, documentary or processing fee, is a charge the dealer sets for preparing the sale paperwork. It is a dealer fee, not a government charge.

You can ask the dealer what the doc fee is for, whether it is required to buy this vehicle, and whether it was included in the price they advertised.

Whether a doc fee is taxed depends on the state. In Florida and North Carolina, otdog's tax rules include a doc fee in the taxable price. In Texas, a separately stated documentary fee is not taxed.
