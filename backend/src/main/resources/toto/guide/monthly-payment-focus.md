A monthly payment depends on the amount financed, the APR and the loan term. A lower payment can come from a longer term, which adds interest, rather than from a lower price.

When a worksheet shows a monthly payment but not the out-the-door total or the term, ask for an itemized out-the-door total and the term in months. otdog does not work out either one from the payment.
