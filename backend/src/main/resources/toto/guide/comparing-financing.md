otdog compares the dealer's financing and your outside offer over their full terms: the monthly payment, the final payment, total interest, scheduled payments and the total transaction cost.

A cost difference is shown only when both scenarios share the same purchase assumptions: the purchase price, out-the-door total, net amount due, cash paid, deferred charges and add-ons. If a rebate depends on dealer financing, settle that with the dealer first.

otdog also shows an APR-only change: your outside APR applied to the dealer's amount financed, term and fees. That is an estimate for comparison, not another loan offer.

For each financed add-on, otdog estimates the extra scheduled payments and interest by removing that one add-on while holding the APR, term and other inputs fixed. The estimate leaves out tax changes and cancellation refunds.
