Before the dealer makes an offer for your trade-in, you can run free checks yourself on other sites. otdog links to two kinds: a value lookup, which gives an estimate from the VIN or license plate, and an instant online offer, which is a written offer to buy the car, good for a set number of days.

These are outside estimates and offers that you gather. otdog does not look them up, check them or vouch for them, and does not send your car's details to either site.

You can type back what you found. An estimate is recorded with where and when you looked it up. An estimate looked up more than 30 days ago is called old; look it up again before relying on it.
