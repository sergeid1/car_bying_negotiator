Texas requires liability coverage of at least 30/60/25: $30,000 of bodily injury liability per person, $60,000 per accident, and $25,000 of property damage liability.

All auto policies in Texas include personal injury protection (PIP) and uninsured motorist coverage. If you don't want either one, you must reject it in writing.

A state minimum is the least coverage the state requires. It is not a suggestion of how much coverage to carry.
