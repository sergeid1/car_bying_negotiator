An itemized out-the-door quote lists the selling price, each dealer fee, any add-ons, taxes, and government charges, each with its own amount. It shows you what you are paying for before you visit the dealership.

otdog drafts a request for an itemized out-the-door quote. You review it, then send it from your own email. otdog never sends it for you.

A good quote names the vehicle, ideally by its VIN, so you know which vehicle it prices. Each line says what it is for. A charge with only a code or an abbreviation is worth a question.
