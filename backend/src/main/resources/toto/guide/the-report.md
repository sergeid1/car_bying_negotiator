The report leads with the out-the-door total, what stands out in the quote, and the next thing to do. It shows dealer fees and add-ons as their own parts of the total.

The report turns its findings into questions you can edit and copy for the dealer. You decide which to ask.

For questions about your own numbers or case, open your report. Toto, the AI guide, cannot see your case.
