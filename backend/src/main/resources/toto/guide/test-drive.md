otdog drafts an email offering the dealer up to three windows of at least an hour. The dealer is offered one-hour times from them and asked to reply with an option number. You approve the message and send it from your own email.

Check each reply yourself. Nothing is booked until you confirm a time and approve the booking message. Nothing is added to the message unless you tick it.

The calendar file includes a reminder, and you also receive a reminder email if appointment emails are on.
