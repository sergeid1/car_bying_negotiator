When a dealer agrees to change a price or remove a charge, asking for a revised itemized quote in writing keeps every change on the record.

If the dealer sends a revised quote, upload it to your Buying Case. The report shows each change between the two versions as a quote change.

A verbal or partial figure is not a written quote. Ask the dealer to confirm it in writing before relying on it.
