A quote usually contains two different kinds of charge beyond the selling price. Government charges, such as title and registration, are collected by the dealer and passed on. Dealer fees and add-ons are set by the dealer.

Knowing which is which tells you which lines the dealer can explain or change. In a clear quote, dealer fees are not mixed in with government charges.

When you upload a quote, otdog sorts each line into dealer fees, add-ons, taxes, government charges and the other parts of the total, and asks you to confirm each amount against its page.
