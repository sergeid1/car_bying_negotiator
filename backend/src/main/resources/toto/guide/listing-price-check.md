You can save the dealer's listing for the car as a screenshot, a saved PDF of the page, or its pasted text. otdog keeps what it advertised: price, VIN, mileage, dealer and any price fine print. otdog never fetches the page itself.

Once you have a dealer offer or desk paperwork, otdog compares its vehicle price with the advertised price. A price that grew between the ad and the paperwork is shown with its dollar difference and one question for the dealer.

The ad's fine print, such as a price that requires dealer financing, can explain part of a price difference.
