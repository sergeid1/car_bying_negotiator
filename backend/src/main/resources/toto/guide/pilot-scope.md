otdog's pilot covers used passenger vehicles bought from licensed dealers by Buyers in three states: Florida, Texas and North Carolina. Your ZIP code sets your state's taxes and fees.

New vehicles, leases, private-party sales, auctions, motorcycles, RVs and commercial vehicles are outside the pilot.
