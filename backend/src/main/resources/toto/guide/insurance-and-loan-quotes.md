otdog is adding a way to compare car insurance quotes and loan pre-approvals from many companies with one application, through a licensed comparison partner. Insurers and lenders make every decision; otdog does not sell insurance or make loans.

Insurance quotes are listed by price for the same coverage, and loans by total cost. A company that pays otdog more does not move up the list.

Loan pre-approvals through otdog are soft inquiries only. A pre-approval you choose can become your outside finance offer, to compare with the dealer's financing at the desk.

An insurance or loan application goes only to the licensed partner and the companies quoting through it, and only when you agree. otdog never sells your information.
