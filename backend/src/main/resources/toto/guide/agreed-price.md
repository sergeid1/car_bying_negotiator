An Agreed Price is created only when you accept a written dealer quote. A verbal or partial figure is not enough; ask the dealer for written confirmation first.

Acceptance is subject to a test drive and final paperwork matching the quote. At the desk, the Desk Check compares the paperwork with your Agreed Price.

You can download a summary of the Agreed Price to view or print and bring to the dealership.
