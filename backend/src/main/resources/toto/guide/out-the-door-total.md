The out-the-door total is what you would pay to drive the vehicle away. A complete quote breaks it into parts, each with its own line: the selling price, rebates and credits, dealer fees, add-ons, taxes, government charges, and a trade-in if you have one.

A monthly payment is not an out-the-door total. Asking for the out-the-door total in writing, with every part on its own line, lets you see what the whole purchase costs before you talk about payments.

In a complete quote, every part has its own line and amount, and the lines add up to the total shown. When you upload a quote, otdog checks that the parts add up to the out-the-door total.
