Payment packing is when the quoted monthly payment is higher than the worksheet's own price, APR, term and down payment produce. The difference may include products not shown on the worksheet.

otdog names possible payment packing when the quoted payment is more than $1 a month above the payment the worksheet's own numbers produce. It shows the monthly difference and that difference over the term, and asks for a reconciliation.

A payment gap does not prove a product was added. Timing, rounding or a different amount financed can also explain it, so the question asks the dealer to show how the payment is worked out.
