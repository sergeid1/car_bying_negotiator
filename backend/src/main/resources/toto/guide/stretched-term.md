A stretched term is a loan term longer than the one you planned. A longer term lowers the monthly payment, but you make more payments and usually pay more interest in total.

When the worksheet's term is longer than the term you stated, otdog shows how much lower the monthly payment is and how much more scheduled interest the longer term adds, with the same amount financed and APR at each term.

At a zero APR, a longer term can lower the payment without adding interest.
