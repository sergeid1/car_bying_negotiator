For a North Carolina resident buying a passenger vehicle from a North Carolina dealer, otdog applies the North Carolina highway use tax of 3% to the taxable price after any trade-in allowance. There is no local tax and no cap for a passenger car.

On an example $30,000 price with no trade-in, the tax is $900.00. With a $12,000 trade-in, $18,000 is taxable and the tax is $540.00.

The taxable price includes accessories attached at delivery and any administrative or documentary fee. A separately stated online registration fee is not taxed, and neither is a separately stated service contract, warranty, maintenance agreement, roadside assistance, GAP or insurance.

North Carolina collects the highway use tax when the car is titled. The dealer may collect it with the sale, or you pay it when you title the car, so a quote can leave it out. It is still owed, and otdog's total includes it.
