The trade-in check compares the dealer's trade allowance with written offers you got for the car elsewhere and with a value range or your own looked-up estimate. It also checks the payoff on the paperwork against the payoff you recorded.

A written offer is an offer to buy your car for a stated amount, good until an expiry date. Enter it in otdog through "Add a written offer", not as an estimate. The check compares the dealer's allowance with your best unexpired written offer. An estimate is never shown as an offer you can take to the dealer.

When the dealer's paperwork shows a trade-in VIN, otdog checks it against the trade-in you recorded.

The check also shows about how much tax the trade-in removes under your state's rules, because selling the car elsewhere gives up that tax saving.
