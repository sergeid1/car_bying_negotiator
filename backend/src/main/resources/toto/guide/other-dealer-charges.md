A market adjustment is an amount the dealer adds on top of the vehicle's price. It goes by many names, such as "Mkt Adj", "ADM", "additional dealer markup", "market value adjustment", a dealer addendum, or a premium package the dealer added. otdog shows it as a price increase on the car, not a service. It is negotiable.

Some charges may repeat a charge already on the paperwork: dealer preparation, a pre-delivery inspection or PDI, reconditioning on a new car, dealer handling, or a second freight or destination line. The manufacturer sets one destination charge and prints it on the window sticker, so a second one is worth asking about.

These charges are set by the dealer, not by a government office. otdog says what each line is and asks; it does not say whether a dealer may charge it.

Questions you can put to the dealer about any of them: What is this charge for? Is it required to buy this vehicle, or optional? Does it repeat another charge on this paperwork? Can it be removed, and what is the out-the-door total without it? Was it included in the price you advertised?
