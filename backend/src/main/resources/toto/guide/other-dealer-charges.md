A preparation or delivery fee is a charge the dealer sets for getting the vehicle ready. A market adjustment is an amount the dealer adds on top of the vehicle's price. Both are set by the dealer, not by a government office.

Questions you can put to the dealer about either one: What is this charge for? Is it required to buy this vehicle, or optional? Can it be removed, and what is the out-the-door total without it? Was it included in the price you advertised?
