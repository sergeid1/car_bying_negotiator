Start with the car or the quote. Describe the car you want, or start from a quote you already have. You do not need an account.

Ask dealers for an itemized out-the-door quote. otdog drafts the request; you send it from your own email.

Upload the dealer's quote, or photograph the paperwork with your phone. otdog reads each amount and outlines where it found it on the page. You confirm or correct every one.

Read the report. See the out-the-door total, what stands out, and the questions to ask the dealer.

When the dealer answers, otdog drafts a reply from the report's figures. You edit it and send it yourself.

At the dealership, photograph the four-square. otdog checks it against the price you agreed, including your trade-in.

otdog leaves a value it could not read missing rather than guessing it, and no analysis runs until you have confirmed the amounts.
