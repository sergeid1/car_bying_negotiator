If you have your own loan offer, otdog compares the dealer's APR with it. When the dealer's APR is higher, otdog shows the extra scheduled interest and the monthly difference, at the worksheet's amount financed and term.

This compares the APR only. It does not compare different lender fees or payment timing, and it does not choose a lender for you.
