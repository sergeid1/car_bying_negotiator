Negative equity is when you owe more on your trade-in than the dealer allows for it. A dealer can add that shortfall to the amount financed on the new loan, which raises the payment and the interest you pay.

otdog names negative equity folded in when the paperwork shows it in the amount financed, even if the worksheet does not label it. Where it can, otdog shows how much it adds a month and in scheduled payments over the term.

A trade-in shortfall is a trade figure, never a discount on the car.
