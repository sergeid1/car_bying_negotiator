otdog suggests price limits for a car from the dealer offer you checked against the paperwork and the market range for that car. Check the offer first if you have not yet.

The limits are worked out by fixed rules, not by an AI model, and you approve them before they are used. A market range is an estimate of selling price from stored evidence.
