Toto is an AI guide, not a person. It answers questions about the car-buying process and about otdog, only from otdog's reviewed guides.

Toto cannot see your case, your report or your documents. For questions about your own numbers or case, open your report.

Please don't type personal details into Toto. Your questions are sent to an AI provider to be answered and are not saved by otdog.

Toto does not answer legal or tax questions, whether to sign, questions about your own numbers, a vehicle's condition or history, or which dealer, lender or insurer to choose.
