Five questions you can put to the dealer about any charge: What is this charge for? Is it set by the dealer or by a government office? Is it required to buy this vehicle, or optional? Can it be removed, and what is the out-the-door total without it? Was it included in the price you advertised?

Any line you do not recognise is worth asking about, including one shown only as a code.

Asking for the answer in writing, on a revised quote, keeps every change on the record.
