otdog is free for you. Today otdog earns no money from anyone.

otdog is adding a way to compare car insurance quotes and loan pre-approvals from many companies with one application, through a licensed comparison partner. The insurance and lending companies you see there, or the partner, may pay otdog. That is how otdog stays free. You never pay otdog anything, whether or not you take an offer.

No dealer, dealer group, or dealer finance office pays otdog, not for leads, placement, appointments, or sales. No company can pay to change a finding, a rating, or anything otdog says about your deal.
