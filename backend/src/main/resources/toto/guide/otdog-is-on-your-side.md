otdog is a tool on your side, the Buyer's. It helps you find cars, check quotes, draft your emails to dealers, and check desk paperwork. You send every email from your own email and make every decision. We don't work with dealers. We help people.

No dealer pays otdog for anything, and no company pays for placement. Nothing about you goes to a dealer unless you send it yourself.

otdog never agrees to buy, signs anything or pays a deposit. Only you can do those things.
