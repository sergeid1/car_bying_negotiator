Florida requires at least $10,000 of personal injury protection (PIP) and $10,000 of property damage liability on a private passenger car policy.

Florida does not require bodily injury liability of most drivers. A driver can be required to carry it after some crashes or convictions.

A state minimum is the least coverage the state requires. It is not a suggestion of how much coverage to carry.
