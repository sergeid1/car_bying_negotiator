The selling price is the price of the vehicle itself, after any discount the dealer shows. It is one part of the out-the-door total, not the whole of it.

Where enough market evidence exists, otdog compares the selling price with a market range for the car. A market range is an estimate of selling price from stored evidence, not an offer from any dealer.
