The trade-in allowance is the amount the dealer gives you for your old car. The payoff is what you still owe on it to your lender. A complete quote shows both on their own lines.

A payoff quote from your lender is valid through a set date. If yours is past its valid-through date, get a current payoff quote before relying on it.

In otdog, you record the trade-in and what is owed on it once. It belongs to your Buying Journey, so every case in the journey uses the same one.
