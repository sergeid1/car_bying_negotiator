For policies written or renewed from July 1, 2025, North Carolina requires at least $50,000 of bodily injury liability per person, $100,000 per accident, and $50,000 of property damage liability.

North Carolina also requires uninsured motorist coverage at the same $50,000 and $100,000 limits, and every new or renewed policy also includes underinsured motorist coverage.

A state minimum is the least coverage the state requires. It is not a suggestion of how much coverage to carry.
