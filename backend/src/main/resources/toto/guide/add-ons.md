Add-ons are products or services added to the sale, such as protection packages or accessories. Some are pre-installed on the vehicle, such as protection coatings, etching, or accessories. Others are optional products, such as service contracts or gap coverage.

In a clear quote, add-ons are listed separately from the selling price, so you can see which ones are optional. You can ask whether each add-on is required to buy this vehicle, and what the out-the-door total is without it.

An add-on that is financed with the car costs more than its cash price, because you pay interest on it over the loan term.
