If you are already at the dealership, "I'm at the dealer" opens the camera first. You photograph the pencil sheet, four-square or buyer's order, and otdog reads the VIN from it.

If the VIN matches the car you agreed on, the paperwork goes to your Desk Check. If it does not, otdog starts a separate case for that car. You do not need an account.

otdog asks only what the paperwork cannot answer: whether there is a trade-in, what is owed on it, and whether you have your own loan offer if the worksheet shows a loan.

You can also type the numbers the four-square or buyer's order shows and leave the rest blank. These typed numbers are compared, not saved.
