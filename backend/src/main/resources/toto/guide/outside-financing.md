An outside finance offer is a loan offer you get yourself, for example from a bank or credit union, rather than through the dealer. It gives you an APR and term to compare with the dealer's financing.

Outside financing is financing you choose, not a dealer discount. It changes what the loan costs, not the price of the car.

In otdog, you can enter one outside finance offer and compare it with the dealer's financing. otdog does not choose a lender for you.
